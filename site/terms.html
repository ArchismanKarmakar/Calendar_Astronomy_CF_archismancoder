Accuracy and safety

Times and dates are computed with established astronomical models and published sources: NASA for eclipses, the International Meteor Organization for meteor showers, and the British Astronomical Association and Seiichi Yoshida for comets. We take care to get them right, but we can't guarantee them.

  • Comet brightness and meteor rates are forecasts and often differ from what actually happens.
  • Local times depend on time-zone and daylight-saving rules, which governments can change.
  • Public holidays come from the open-source python-holidays project. Holidays that depend on moon sighting are marked "(est.)". Official dates, regional holidays and observed days can differ, so check official sources before you rely on one.
  • Visibility notes are computed for the calendar's reference city and can differ elsewhere in the same time zone.

Eye safety. Never look at the Sun, even during a partial eclipse, without a certified solar filter (ISO 12312-2) or a safe projection method. Never point binoculars, telescopes or cameras at the Sun without a proper front-mounted solar filter. Serious eye damage can happen in seconds.

Don't use the calendar for navigation, legal deadlines or anything where a mistake could cause harm.

Privacy

There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking cookies.

  • In your browser. Calendars are built on your device. Your settings are kept in your browser's local storage so they're there next time; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.
  • Our hosting. The site runs on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy.
  • Images. When you choose ESO or ESA/Hubble images, our server fetches them from the providers for you. We don't keep our own record of who asked for which image, but Cloudflare's request logs for the site (which we use to spot errors and abuse) can show which image was requested.
  • Code libraries. The calendar engine and PDF preview load from public code networks (jsDelivr and cdnjs), which receive your IP address as part of delivering those files.
  • Donations are processed by the payment providers described above.
